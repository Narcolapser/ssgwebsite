# Rise of Industry

## What could have been done better

### Lack of Indications

More than once in the game I was frustrated by things not happening when I thought they would be and there was either no indication or poor indication of why. This is a UI issue and as such it is not some where I have any skill in saying how to remediate the issue but I'll give some examples.

First a poor indication was when a wear house would not send materials to another ware house. I had been having issues with trains and their routing so I thought maybe I had that wrong. But every now and then a train would go so that wasn't the issue. There was an indicator in the bottom right of my screen piled in with 40 some other warnings (I had just purchased a competitor and was still sorting out their issues) so I didn't see "Destination storage full" was the issue. Perhaps an indicator on the actual logistics route as to why the route could not send at that moment instead of in an unrelated warning area.

Second was the root cause of the above issue. My primary ware house was set to sell resources to the state when the reached a certain threshold. Despire having a full warehouse nothing was being sold to the state, no indication as to why. It took me a while but I have a theory as to what the issue is. I think that warehouses have a maximum number of trucks that they can have out at any one given moment. I was noticing that the number of trucks going out was exactly matching the backed up queue of trucks coming in. Again, some indication that I had reached the limit of what this warehouse could manage would have been nice. 

### Crashing

When I opened the game it would frequently crash when I clicked "load" or "continue". Typically it would crash twice then the third time I could actually load my game. Some times the game crashed while I was doing something challenging like laying a track across the entire map. Once it crashed while saving. Never a good look.

### Artificial bottlenecks

A reasonable person would have one warehouse per city. But I got the feeling that such rapidly becomes not a reasonable strategy as with my issue above I seemed to have run out of trucks. You could say I was managing the return to my warehouse poorly but at the end of the day there is a single entrance to that warehouse. My blockage was not further up the road, it was at the entrace to the warehouse itself. This was true of basically everywhere. It was particularly annoying at places that historically were tended directly be trains link mines. I had little outposts that were a mine of some sort, a ware house, and a train terminal. All connected by roads. The roads were entirely un-necessary if I could just deliver straight into the warehouse/train terminal. That may sound a little cheaty, but that's how the warehouses and the train terminals work. You don't have trucks driving between the warehouse and the train terminal. 

### Limitations of the train system

I'm a big fan of Factorio and it has become my gold standard for train systems. Not that it doesn't have its flaws but it has a lot that it has done right. One thing I was feeling the lack of in this game is signals. I was annoyed a lot by a "T" intersection I made where the trains on the from the top left were traveling in, dropping down to travel accross the tiny patch of rail in the lower half of the T where trains from the right were coming from, and then going back up to the top were they started. It added tiles for no reason what so ever and caused a lot of congestion by making trains run into each other. Had I had signals like I do in factorio I could have enforced one-way roads and prevented the right bound trains from doing that. Which, by the way, there are one way streets, so one way rails would have been a nice addition. There were other issues I had with the train system. It was the exact wrong mix of simplified and complex. I'd either ratch up the things you are able to do to shape your train logistics or ratch down the micro management.
