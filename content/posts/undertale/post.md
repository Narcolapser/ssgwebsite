# Game Review - Undertale

Oh lordy. This game is quite the study in a lot of things. I have heard a lot about undertale so I knew what I was getting when I went into it. But I tried to play it as if I hadn't heard "It's the game were you don't have to kill anybody." and just approached it as a Earthbound homage. 

There are a lot of things I could say about the game. At time of writing I've only played it through once on the "Nuetral" path. One of the big things I want to point out is how it's not really a game, it's more of an interactive novel. The game gives you the guise of choice in a lot of places, but really you are mostly bound to a certain path. This kind of bothered me, or bored me, but I can't really say it is a negative. This is a stylistic/genre choice. While I was expecting Earthbound it was a disappointment, but that disappointment came from misplace expectations, not poor quality of game. Once I had rejiggered my head into that mentality the story was more enjoyable. 

The art style drew a lot from the NES era of games, but with a certain level of bizaarness to it. The creatures ranged from silly frogs to nightmare fuel. The sense of humor was very much so in your face. The game all but grabs you by the shoulders to yell at you "I KNOW WHO YOU ARE ANY WHY YOU ARE HERE!" It is a solid critique of the type of games where you go around murdering everything (Though I'll point out in some of those games it specifically says things like "XYZ fled" when you finish the fight).

Just about anything I think of as a negative of the game I would chalk up to being an artistic choice. The graphics could have been better, but that was a choice to make them the way they did. The game could have been longer, but why over stay your welcome? It's a hard game to pin down in review.
