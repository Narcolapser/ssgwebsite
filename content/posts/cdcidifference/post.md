# CD/CI difference in kind

I'm in a good position to compare companies with a CDCI pipeline and those without. I was an employee of USD not two months ago, and the only CDCI stuff they had I setup, there was but one. Right now I am reading some documentation for a new tool that my new employer, CE, wants to make use of. And in reading it I was struck with a comparison. 

It is easy to assume, and marketing folks will tell you, that having a CDCI pipeline changes everything. This is false. The analogy that struck me this morning is it is a little bit like electrifying something like a flour mill. An electric flower mill principle does the same thing as one that runs off a water wheel or wind mill. They grind grain into flower. But the problems they solve to do such are different. Recently toured a griss mill in Nebraska that used water power to turn it's wheels back in the day. The basement of the building had fly wheels and axles and chains and betls all for powering the machinary upstairs. This works, it works a lot better than people give it credit for. But when your mill is electrified, you aren't worrying about all those mechanics, your focus can change to individual machines. When your electric you don't have to worry about whether there is a river near where you want to build, or if it was a dry spring. At the end of the day you are still doing the same thing, grinding grain, but the tools you use and what you spend your time on differs.

Thus it is with CDCI. At the end of the day, you are still building and deploying code, that doesn't change. But what does change is what you spend your time doing. You spend less time ensuring your libraries are right on all the machines and moving code around. You start a build and you walk away to do something else. And because deploying is fast and easy, you make lots of smaller changes instead of gathering a week's worth of changes to deploy at once. The focus of your work shifts in ways that are hard to quantify. The product I'm looking into right now is "sonarqube" which monitors changes for quality, bugs, security, maintainability, etc. This can be done without a CDCI pipeline, but with one it is just a quick tack on in comparison.
